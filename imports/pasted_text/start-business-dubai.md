Work with local clients
No restrictions on business area
🟩 2. Freezone Company
100% foreign ownership

Limited UAE operations

🟩 Step 1: Choose Your Business Activity

Decide what type of business you want to start.

Examples:

Trading
Consultancy
E-commerce
Services

👉 Your activity will decide your license type.

🟩 Step 2: Select Company Structure

Choose:

Mainland
Freezone
Offshore

👉 This affects your operations and permissions.

🟩 Step 3: Choose Business Name

Select a unique company name.

Make sure:

It follows UAE rules
It is not already registered
It reflects your business
🟩 Step 4: Apply for Initial Approval

You need approval from authorities to start your business process.

🟩 Step 5: Prepare Legal Documents

Prepare documents such as:

Application forms
Business details
Identification documents
🟩 Step 6: Choose Office Location

Depending on your company type:

Mainland → physical office required
Freezone → flexible options
🟩 Step 7: Apply for Business License

This is the most important step.

You will receive:

Trade license
Legal permission to operate
🟩 Step 8: Apply for Visa

After license approval, you can apply for:

Investor visa
Employee visas
🟩 Step 9: Open Business Bank Account

You need a bank account for:

Transactions
Business operations
🟩 Step 10: Start Your Business

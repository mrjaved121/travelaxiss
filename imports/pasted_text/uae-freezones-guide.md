🟨 H2: What is a Freezone in UAE?

A freezone is a special economic area in UAE where businesses can operate with certain benefits.

Each freezone is managed by its own authority and has its own rules.

Freezones are designed to:

Attract foreign investors
Support startups
Simplify business setup
🟩 Key Features of Freezone Companies
100% foreign ownership
Easy registration process
Business-friendly policies
Access to international markets

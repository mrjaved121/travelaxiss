🟨 H2: Basic Documents Required for Company Formation in UAE

Let’s start with the most common documents required.

🟩 1. Passport Copy

This is the most important document.

Requirements:

Clear and valid passport copy
Must not be expired
All partners/shareholders must provide
🟩 2. Visa Copy (If Available)

If you are already in UAE:

Provide current visa copy

If not:

You can still apply for company formation
🟩 3. Passport Size Photos

You will need:

Recent photos
White background
Clear quality
🟩 4. Business Activity Details

You must clearly define:

What your business will do
Type of services or products

👉 This helps authorities issue the correct license.

🟩 5. Application Form

You will need to fill:

Business registration forms
Authority-specific forms
🟨 H2: Documents Required for Mainland Company Formation

Mainland companies require additional documentation because they operate within UAE markets.

🟩 1. Trade Name Approval

You must:

Select a unique business name
Get approval from authorities
🟩 2. Initial Approval Certificate

This document confirms that:
👉 You can proceed with business setup

🟩 3. Memorandum of Association (MOA)

MOA defines:

Business structure
Ownership details
Responsibilities

👉 This is a very important legal document.

🟩 4. Office Tenancy Contract (Ejari)

Mainland companies must have:

Physical office
Registered tenancy contract
🟩 5. External Approvals (If Required)

Some activities need approvals from:

Municipality
Health department
Other authorities
🟨 H2: Documents Required for Freezone Company Formation

Freezone companies have a simpler process.

🟩 1. Passport Copy

Same requirement as mainland.

🟩 2. Application Form

Filled and submitted to freezone authority.

🟩 3. Business Plan (For Some Activities)

Some freezones require:

Short business plan
Activity explanation
🟩 4. NOC (No Objection Certificate)

If you are already employed in UAE:
👉 You may need NOC from your employer

🟩 5. Address Details

Some freezones may require:

Residential address
Contact details

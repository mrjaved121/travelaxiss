🟨 H2: What is a UAE Visa?

A UAE visa is a legal permit that allows a person to:

Enter the UAE
Stay for a specific period
Work or do business

There are different types of visas depending on your purpose.

🟢 1. Tourist Visa

For short visits and tourism.

🟢 2. Visit Visa

For family visits or short stays.

🟢 3. Employment Visa

For people working in UAE companies.

🟢 4. Investor Visa

For business owners and investors.

🟢 5. Freelance Visa

For independent professionals.

🟢 6. Residency Visa

Allows long-term stay in UAE.

Once your entry permit is approved:

After entering UAE:

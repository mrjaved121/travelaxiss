🟨 H2: What is a Local Sponsor in UAE?

A local sponsor is a UAE national who was previously required to:

Hold shares in a mainland company
Represent the company legally

In older rules:

Foreigners could not fully own mainland companies

👉 This is no longer the case for many business activities.

🟨 H2: Can You Start a Business Without Local Sponsor?

👉 YES — you can now start a business without a local sponsor.

Many activities allow 100% foreign ownership

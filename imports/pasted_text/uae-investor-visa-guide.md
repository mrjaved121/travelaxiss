🟨 H2: What is an Investor Visa in UAE?

An investor visa is a type of residency visa that allows foreign nationals to live in the UAE based on their investment or business ownership.

With an investor visa, you can:

Live legally in UAE
Start and manage your business
Open a bank account
Sponsor your family

👉 It is one of the best options for entrepreneurs who want long-term presence in UAE.

🟨 H2: Who Can Apply for Investor Visa in UAE?

You can apply for an investor visa if:

You start a company in UAE
You invest in an existing business
You become a partner/shareholder in a company
You own property (in some cases)

👉 This visa is designed for people who want to build business or invest in UAE.

🟨 H2: Step-by-Step Process to Get Investor Visa in UAE

Let’s break down the process in simple steps.

🟩 Step 1: Start Your Business

First, you need to:

Register your company
Get trade license

👉 Without business registration, you cannot apply for investor visa.

🟩 Step 2: Apply for Entry Permit

You must apply for an entry permit.

This allows you to:

Enter UAE
Start visa processing
🟩 Step 3: Entry into UAE

If you are outside UAE:
👉 Enter using your entry permit

🟩 Step 4: Medical Test

You must complete:

Medical fitness test

👉 This is required for residency visa.

🟩 Step 5: Emirates ID Application

Apply for Emirates ID.

👉 This is your official identification in UAE.

🟩 Step 6: Visa Stamping

Final step:
👉 Your visa is stamped in your passport

Now you are legally a resident of UAE.
